## FACT — Etsy AI + likeness (not a badge)

Etsy **allows** seller-prompted AI. It **requires** disclosure **in the listing description** if AI was used. Official permitted example is exactly this pond: *“a custom portrait of a buyer’s pet generated using AI tools.”* Attribution is **Designed by**, not Made by / Handmade. Sources: [Seller Handbook — AI creations](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004), [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/) (seller-prompted AI; last noted update 10 Jun 2025), [Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/sellers/) §5 (“If an item is created through the use of artificial intelligence, you must disclose this in your relevant listings”).

**What actually gets dinged** (automated systems + member/third-party flags; listing removal; repeat or related-account suspend):

1. **Missing AI disclosure** on a generative item.  
2. **Handmade / Made-by** on an AI item.  
3. **Celebrity / copyrighted character** likeness (superhero, yellow-character, princess/IP themes).  
4. **Privacy / publicity** of real people (buyer-sent faces still sit on the seller; other people’s faces without rights).  
5. **Jun 2025 “original design”** cut — template / purchased-prompt SKUs.  
6. Custom listing **hero image** that is a blank / “your photo here” instead of a finished example.

Bruce lock = we will **not** put the required sentence in the listing. That is a **willful** miss of (1), not a paperwork miss. **Lethal only when Imagine is generative and the SKU is a portrait people already report as AI** (digital caricature / watercolor / cartoon face printables). Pure photo→chart quantization (Pic2Pat-class) is **not** that rule. Do not use this FACT to argue for an “AI used” badge.
