FinCEN Assesses Historic $125 Million Penalty Against UBS Financial Services Inc.

WASHINGTON—The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) today announced
a historic $125 million civil money penalty against UBS Financial Services Inc. for violations
of the Bank Secrecy Act.
