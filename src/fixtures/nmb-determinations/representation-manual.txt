                           NATIONAL MEDIATION BOARD
                            REPRESENTATION MANUAL




                        Revised Text Effective July 22, 2024


                                      NOTICE

This Manual provides general procedural guidance to the National Mediation Board’s
staff with respect to the processing of representation cases before the NMB. Such
procedural guidance is not required by or subject to the Administrative Procedure
Act. The provisions of this Manual are neither obligatory upon the Members of the
Board nor do they constitute the exclusive procedure for the NMB’s investigation of
representation matters pursuant to the Railway Labor Act.



                                             Maria-Kate Dowling
                                             General Counsel
