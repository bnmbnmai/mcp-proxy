8. CHARGE
BUSINESS IN WHICH ENGAGED:
Workhorse Brewing Company, Inc, ("Proponent") operates a brewery.
VIOLATIONS:
TTB alleges that Proponent failed to report the change in proprietorship resulting from a conversion from a LLC to a C-Corporation in
violation of 26 U.S.C. §5401(a) and 27 C.F.R. §25.72.
TTB alleges that Proponent failed to remit Federal Excise Tax on beer removed for consumption or sale in violation of 26 U.S.C. §5051; 27
C.F.R. §25.153.
TTB alleges that Proponent failed to file Federal Excise Tax Returns on Federal Excise Tax incurred on beer removed for consumption in
violation of 26 U.S.C. §5061; 27 C.F.R. §25.164(a).
TTB alleges that Proponent failed to timely file Federal Excise Tax Returns and timely pay Federal Excise Taxes, thus, subjected to
penalties and interest under 26 U.S.C. §§ 6651(a)(1), 6651(a)(2), and 6601.
DATE OR PERIOD AND LOCATION OF VIOLATIONS:

19406.
