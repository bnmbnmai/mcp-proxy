                        THE COMMISSION’S JURISDICTION
                          AND REQUIREMENTS OF LAW

3.   Under 46 U.S.C. § 41302(a), the Commission is empowered to investigate any conduct, fee,

     or charge that the Commission believes to be in violation of the Shipping Act of 1984, as

     amended, 46 U.S.C. §§ 40101-41310 (Shipping Act).

4.   Effective June 16, 2022, the Ocean Shipping Reform Act of 2022 (OSRA 2022) (P.L. 117-

     146) amended the Shipping Act to set forth, inter alia, new provisions at 46 U.S.C. § 41310,

     entitled Charge Complaints.

5.   The provisions of 46 U.S.C. § 41310 require the Commission to “promptly investigate”

     charges assessed by a common carrier for compliance with 46 U.S.C. §§ 41104(a) and

     41102.

6.   If the Commission determines that a charge does not comply with §§ 41104(a) or 41102,

     the Commission shall, under 46 U.S.C. § 41310(c), “promptly” order the refund of charges

     paid or waiver of charges assessed.

7.   If the Commission determines that a charge does not comply with §§ 41104(a) or 41102,

     the Commission also is authorized under 46 U.S.C. § 41310(d) to issue a civil penalty under

     46 U.S.C. §§ 41107 and 41109 to the common carrier making such charge.
