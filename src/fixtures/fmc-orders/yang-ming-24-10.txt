I.     INTRODUCTION

       A.     Overview

       Complainant TZ SSE Buyer, LLC (“Complainant”) 2 filed an amended complaint alleging
that Respondent Yang Ming Marine Transport Corp. (“Respondent”) violated the Shipping Act
of 1984, as amended, by charging Complainant unjust and unreasonable detention and
demurrage in violation of 46 U.S.C. § 41102(c) and refusing to deal or negotiate detention and
demurrage charges in violation of 46 U.S.C. § 41104(a)(10). In short, Complainant argues that
Respondent imposed a combined total of 705 unjust and unreasonable demurrage, detention, per
diem and yard storage fees, spread across 626 container shipments (some container shipments
had multiple different types of charges) from 2021 through 2022. The aggregate total of
allegedly unjust and unreasonable demurrage, detention, per diem and yard storage fees is
$1,304,526.

         Impact Products, LLC and Safety Zone, LLC (collectively, “Supply Source”) filed an
initial Verified Complaint that was served by the Commission on February 14, 2024. Supply
Source eventually filed for bankruptcy, and Complainant bought Supply Source’s assets. By
means of the Verified Amended Complaint, TZ SSE Buyer, LLC was substituted as

        As discussed below, I find that the Commission lacks jurisdiction over claims related to
381 of the 626 container shipments because the record lacks evidence that the containers were
shipped overseas from a foreign port to a port in the United States. Accordingly, the claims
related to those 381 container shipments are dismissed. The container shipments related to the
dismissed claims are identified in Appendix A, lines 247 to 627. 3

        As for claims associated with the remaining 245 container shipments, Complainant
presents insufficient evidence to meet its burden of proving that Respondent violated either 46
U.S.C. § 41102(c) or 46 U.S.C. § 41104(a)(10). The only exceptions are that Complainant
carries its burden of demonstrating that the demurrage charges related to container no.
TCNU2329916 from January 7, 2021, through January 12, 2021, and container no.
SEGU6841518 for June 23, 2022, unjustly and unreasonably violated § 41102(c). Otherwise,
Complainant’s remaining claims are denied. As for the demurrage charges related to container
nos. TCNU2329916 and SEGU6841518, Complainant is entitled to $517.50 in reparations.

       B.      Procedural History

        The original Complainants, Impact Products, LLC and Safety Zone, LLC (“Supply
Source”) filed a Complaint dated February 6, 2024, naming Yang Ming Marine Transport Corp.,
as a respondent. (See Complaint).

        On February 14, 2024, the Secretary issued a Notice of Filing of Complaint and
Assignment (“Notice of Filing”), which, among other things, indicated that the Initial Decision
of the presiding judge was to be issued by February 14, 2025. (Notice of Filing, at 2).

         The matter was assigned to Chief Administrative Law Judge Erin M. Wirth, who entered
the Initial Order on February 29, 2024.

       Respondent filed its Answer to Complaint with Affirmative Defenses on March 7, 2024.

      On March 22, 2024, the parties filed a Joint Status Report and Proposed Discovery
Schedule (“JSR”). In the JSR, the parties proposed a case schedule, including discovery cutoff of
August 12, 2024, and completion of briefing by November 9, 2024.

        On March 25, 2024, Chief Judge Wirth issued a Scheduling Order that largely adopted
the parties’ proposed case schedule, with some adjustments.
