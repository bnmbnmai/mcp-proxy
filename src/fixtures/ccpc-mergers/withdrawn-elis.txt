DETERMINATION OF MERGER NOTIFICATION M/25/050 – ELIS/OCL LAUNDRY SERVICES
Section 21 of the Competition Act 2002
Dated 16 December 2025
The Commission is unable, at this stage, to make a determination that the result of the
Proposed Transaction will not be to substantially lessen competition.
Accordingly, the Commission intends to carry out a full investigation under section 22 of the Act.
The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, in accordance with section 21(2)(b)
has determined that it intends to carry out a full investigation under section 22 of the Act.
Elis Textile Services Limited has informed the CCPC that it will not proceed with the proposed
acquisition of O.C.L. Laundry Services Limited and is withdrawing the notification.
The CCPC has closed its investigation and no final determination will be issued.
Outcome: Withdrawn
