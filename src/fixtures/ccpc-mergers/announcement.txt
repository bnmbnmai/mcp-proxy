MERGER ANNOUNCEMENT M/26/006 – UNITED HARDWARE/DERMOT KEHOE SUPPLY & DIY
Section 21 of the Competition Act 2002
Competition and Consumer Protection Commission clears proposed acquisition
whereby United Hardware Designated Activity Company, through its wholly owned
subsidiary Ardentia Limited, would acquire sole control of Kehoe’s Homevalue Limited.
06 March 2026
The Commission will publish the reasons for its determination on its website no later than 60 working days.
