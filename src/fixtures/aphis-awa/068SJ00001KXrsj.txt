2.31(c)(7)               Critical
Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC).
82 naked mole rats were euthanized by a method not on the approved protocol between 9/26/2025 and 10/3/2025. The
naked mole rats were euthanized by CO2 instead of the injectable euthanasia method approved in the protocol. Naked
mole rats are a hypoxia tolerant species, and the use of CO2 required a prolonged exposure of ~40 minutes to achieve
death.
Failure to follow the approved protocol and making unapproved changes does not allow the IACUC to review these
changes as required and ensure the animals involved do not experience negative impacts to their health and welfare.
The IACUC must ensure that all significant changes regarding the care and use of animals in ongoing activities including
euthanasia are reviewed and approved prior to implementation.

Corrective action was implemented prior to this inspection.
To be corrected from this day forward, July 7, 2026
2.33(b)(4)               Critical
Attending veterinarian and adequate veterinary care.
A rabbit died during an anesthetic procedure on 12/11/2025. The animal’s depth of anesthesia had become too light and
the anesthetic gas level was increased. The level was not reduced once a deeper plane of anesthesia was achieved and
the animal subsequently died during the procedure and was not able to be resuscitated.
Failure to appropriately monitor patients and adjust anesthetic levels during a procedure can lead to injury or death of the
patient.
Each research facility must establish and maintain programs of adequate veterinary care that provide adequate guidance
to personnel involved in the care and use of animals regarding handling, immobilization, anesthesia. analgesia,
tranquilization, and euthanasia.
Corrective action was implemented prior to this inspection.
To be corrected from this day forward, July 7, 2026



This inspection and exit interview were conducted with facility representative.
