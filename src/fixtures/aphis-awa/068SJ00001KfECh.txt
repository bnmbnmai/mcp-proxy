2.33(b)(3)
Attending veterinarian and adequate veterinary care.
During a review of facility records, the following non-compliance involving a lack of timely communication of health
findings to the attending veterinarian was observed. On February 17, 2026, a calf was received for a heart study. The
following 2 days, animal care staff reported blood in the feces to the veterinary resident twice but no physical exam or
testing was performed and the attending veterinarian was not notified. Care staff observed additional symptoms including
restlessness, vocalization, and loss of appetite on the 3rd and 4th day after arrival which was again not reported to the
attending veterinarian. On the 5th day after arrival (February 23) the calf was observed to have lost approximately 8lb. It
was not until this point when diagnostics were performed that the attending veterinarian was notified of the health
problems in this animal. Treatment was provided and the calf's symptoms improved but the animal was euthanized due to
being a poor candidate for the study.
Failure to communicate health problems to the attending veterinarian in a timely manner may result in unnecessary
discomfort in the animals.
The facility must establish and maintain a program of veterinary care that includes daily observations with a mechanism of
direct and frequent communication so that timely and accurate information on problems of animal health are conveyed to
the attending veterinarian.
Correct by: Corrected at the time of inspection. Continue to ensure animal health problems are communicated to the
attending veterinarian in a timely manner from 7/14/26 forward.
2.38(f)(1)
Miscellaneous.
During a review of facility records, the following non-compliance involving animal handling was observed. On February 14,
2026, a rabbit was found in a housing room outside its cage. It was discovered the 3rd level cage door was unlatched and
open. The rabbit was diagnosed with a fractured nail on the right forelimb. The rabbit was treated with anti-inflammatory
medications and recovered without incident.
Failure to handle animals in a careful manner, including failing to secure cage latches to keep animals contained to their
enclosures, may result in injury to the animals.
Handling of all animals must be done as carefully as possible in a manner that does not cause trauma or physical harm.
Correct by: Corrected at the time of inspection. Continue to ensure animals are handled in a careful manner from 7/14/26
forward.

3.127(b)
Facilities, outdoor.
During a review of facility records, the following non-compliance involving lack of shelter from inclement weather was
observed. On November 25, 2025, three sheep were inadvertently locked out of the barn with only access to the paddock
and pasture for approximately 22 hours. During this period, the animals did not have access to shelter. The animals also
did not have access to water but there were no signs of dehydration observed. The door between the outdoor area and
the barn is normally kept open but was found closed, blocking the animals' access inside.
Failure to provide shelter can result in discomfort to the animals.
Natural or artificial shelter shall be provided for all animals kept outdoors to afford them protection and to prevent
discomfort to such animals.
Correct by: Corrected at the time of inspection. Continue to ensure all animals housed outdoors have access to shelter
from 7/14/26 forward.
