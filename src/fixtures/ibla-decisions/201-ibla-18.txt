                                        SUMMARY

        The Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977 (SMCRA or the Act)
requires parties seeking to conduct surface coal mining to obtain a permit and to post a
bond guaranteeing their lawful performance. In Tennessee, OSMRE is responsible for
inspecting coal mining operations and providing permittees with notice of and an
opportunity to abate identified violations of governing law. Where a permittee refuses or
is unable to abate the violations as directed, or otherwise fails to comply with their
permit, OSMRE initiates the bond forfeiture process by providing notice of its intent to
forfeit and an opportunity for the permittee or its surety to avoid forfeiture through
various means. Here, the Permittees do not dispute that they failed to abate the
violations on which the 2023 Decisions are predicated, after multiple notices, multiple
abatement opportunities, and the passage of months or even years. We find Permittees’
argument that they have not “refused to” abate the relevant violations insufficient to
carry their burden to demonstrate error on the record presented. Otherwise, Permittees
offer only conclusory and unsupported legal assertions or challenges to matters not
properly before us in these appeals. Accordingly, we affirm OSMRE’s 2023 Decisions.
