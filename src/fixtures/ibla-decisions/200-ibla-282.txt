                OPINION BY CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE IDZIOREK

       On September 10, 2025, the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) transferred to
the Board Eric Weiland’s notice of appeal and request for a stay of a July 30, 2025,
decision issued by the BLM Colorado State Office. 1 In its decision, the BLM declared the
Bella Blue mining claim, serial number CO106324332, null and void ab initio because it
was located entirely within patented lands closed to mineral entry. 2 We docketed the
appeal as IBLA 2025-0344. As we explain in this decision, because Weiland did not file
the appeal directly with the Board as required by regulation, we dismiss it.

                                       BACKGROUND

       To appeal a BLM decision, an appellant must file a notice of appeal with the
Board no later than 30 days after the date of receiving it. 3 The regulations state that no
extension of time will be granted to file a notice of appeal: “If a notice of appeal is filed

       Weiland received the BLM’s decision on August 2, 2025. 5 Weiland therefore had

                                         ANALYSIS

               We Dismiss Weiland’s Appeal for Failure to File with the Board

      Weiland had until September 2, 2025, to file a notice of appeal with the Board. 9
Although we now have a copy of Weiland’s notice of appeal and we assigned it a docket
number, we did not receive it from Weiland. Instead, we learned of the attempt to
appeal from the BLM, which forwarded Weiland’s notice on September 10, 2025. 10

       We cannot consider Weiland’s appeal unless Weiland complied with the
regulations governing the filing of appeals to this Board. The regulations establish the
location and time for filing a notice of appeal, and these requirements are jurisdictional;
we cannot waive them. 11 This is true even if we receive a copy of an appeal from another
