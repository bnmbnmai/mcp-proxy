                                      HI.     ALLEGATIONS

                                  Statutory and Regulatory Background

        3.1.    Pursuant to Section l 2(a)(l)(E) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § l 36j(a)(l)(E), it shall be

unlawful for any person in any State to distribute or sell to any person any pesticide which is

adulterated or misbranded.

        3.2.    Section 2(s) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § 136(s), defines a "person" as "any individual,

partnership, association, corporation, or any organized group of persons whether incorporated or

not."

        3.3.    Section 2(aa) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § 136(aa), defines "State" as "a State, the

District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, Guam, the Trust

Territory of the Pacific Islands, and American Samoa."

        3.4.    Section 2(gg) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C § I 36(gg), defines "to distTibute or sell" as "to

distribute, sell, offer for sale, hold for distribution, hold for sale, hold for shipment, ship, deliver

for shipment, release for shipment, or receive and (having so received) deliver or offer to

deliver."

        3.5.    The regulation at 40 C. F.R. § 152.3 further defines "distribute or sell" as "the acts

of d istributing, sell ing, offering for sale, holding for sale, shipping, holding for shipment,

del ivering for shipment, or receiving and (having so received) del ivering or offering to del iver,

         3.6.    Section 2(a) ofFIFRA, 7 U.S.C. l36(a), defines an "active ingredient,'' in part, as

"in the case of a pesticide ... an ingredient which will prevent, destroy, repel, or mitigate any

pest."

         3.7.    Section 2(u) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § J36(u), defines a "pesticide," in part, as "any

substance or mixture of substances intended for preventing, destroying, repelling, or mitigating

any pest."

         3.8.    The regulation at 40 C.F.R. § 152.3 further defines a "pesticide" as "any

substance or mixture of substances intended for preventing, destroying, repelling, or mitigating

any pest, or intended for use as a plant regulator, defoliant, or desiccant," subject to limited

exceptions.

         3.9.    The regulation at 40 C.F.R. § 152.3 defines a "pesticide product" as "a pesticide

in the particular form (including composition, packaging, and labeling) in which the pesticide is,

or is intended to be, distributed or sold. The term includes any physical apparatus used to deliver

or apply the pesticide if distributed or sold with the pesticide."

         3.10.   Section 2(p) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § 136(p), defines "label" as "the written, printed,

or graphic matter on, or attached to, the pesticide or device or any of its containers or wrappers"

This section also defines " labeling" as "all labels and all other written, printed, or graphic matter

(A) accompanying the pesticide or device at any time; or (B) to which reference is made on the

label or in literature accompanying the pesticide or device."

         3.11.   Under Section 2(q)(l)(D) of FIFRA, 7 U.S.C. § 136(q)(l)(D), a pesticide is

misbranded if, "its label does not bear the registration number assigned under section 7 to each

establishment in which it was produced."

       3.13.    The regulation at 40 C.F.R. § 156.1 0(a)( I), in relevant part, requires every

40 C.F. R. § 156.1 0(t); an iDgredient statement per 40 C.F.R. § 156. 10(g); hazard and

precautionary statements per 40 C.F.R. Part 156, Subparts D and E; and the directions for use per

40 C.F.R. § 156.l0(i).

       3.14.    The regulation at40 C.F.R. § 156.10(a)(4) requires the label to appear on or be

securely attached to the immediate container of the pesticide product.

       3.15.    Under Section 2(q)(l)(F), 7 U.S.C. § 136(q)(l)(F), a pesticide is misbranded if

"the labeling accompanying it does not contain directions for use which arc necessary for

effecting the purpose for which the product is intended and if complied with, together with any

requirements imposed under [FlFRA Section 3(d)], are adequate to protect health and the

environment."

       3.16.    Under Section 2(q)(l)(G), 7 U.S.C. § 136(q)(l)(G), a pesticide is misbranded if

" the label does not contain a warn ing or caution statement which may be necessary and if

complied with, together with any requirements imposed under [FIFRA Section 3(d)], is adequate

to protect health and the environment."
