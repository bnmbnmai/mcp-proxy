        On May 18, 2026 appellant, through counsel, filed a timely appeal from a November 18,
2025 nonmerit decision of the Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs (OWCP). The Clerk
of the Appellate Boards docketed the appeal as No. 26-0527.2

        On April 9, 2009 appellant, then a 44-year-old letter carrier, filed a traumatic injury claim
(Form CA-1) alleging that on that date she injured her left ankle, right knee, and right elbow when
she slipped and fell on an uneven sidewalk while in the performance of duty. She stopped work
on April 10, 2009 and returned to work on April 15, 2009. OWCP accepted the claim for left ankle
sprain and tenosynovitis, contusions of the left elbow and knee, left plantar fasciitis, and abrasions
without infection of the right leg, elbow, and forearm. It paid appellant wage-loss compensation
