CIVIL LIABILITY ORDER R3-2026-0033 TO RESOLVE PERMIT VIOLATIONS
Attached is Administrative Civil Liability Order R3-2026-0033 (ACL Order) signed by the
California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Coast Region (Central Coast
Water Board) Executive Officer. The ACL Order resolves mandatory minimum penalties
for the City Watsonville’s (Discharger) violations of effluent limitations contained in
Waste Discharge Requirements (WDR) Order R3-2014-0006, National Pollutant
Discharge Elimination System Permit (NPDES) CA0048216, that occurred from August
27, 2024, to August 31, 2024, as shown in the notice of violation (NOV) attached to the
ACL Order. Central Coast Water Board staff publicly noticed the Acceptance of
Conditional Settlement Offer and Waiver of Right to Hearing (Acceptance and Waiver)
from April 21, 2026, to May 21, 2026, and received no comments.

As provided in the ACL Order, the Discharger is subject to a total payment amount of
$6,000 (Penalty Amount). No later than June 25, 2026,1 the Discharger must submit
payment to the State Water Resources Control Board (State Water Board) State Water
Pollution Cleanup and Abatement Account2 by taking the following actions:

    1. Pay $6,000 by check payable to “State Water Pollution Cleanup and Abatement
       Account,” noting “ACL Order R3-2026-0033” on the check, and mail to:

       State Water Board Accounting Office
       Attention: ACL Payment
