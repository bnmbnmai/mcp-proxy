                                             FINAL DETERMINATION

    This case has been fully submitted by the parties. For the reasons that follow, the Copyright Claims Board

(“Board”) finds Respondent American Ranch Horse Association, Inc. (“ARHA” or “Respondent”) liable for

infringement and awards Claimant Keith F. Bell (“Bell” or “Claimant”) $750 in statutory damages.


    I.       Procedural History

    Claimant Keith F. Bell (“Bell” or “Claimant”) filed the initial claim in this proceeding on October 4, 2023 (Dkt.

1), and filed the operative amended claim (“Claim”) (Dkt. 3) on October 19, 2023. ARHA filed its Response on

April 3, 2024. (Dkt. 15).

    After Bell was ordered to produce discovery regarding potential damages (Dkt. 23), discovery closed on

September 6, 2024, and the Board ordered the parties to file written testimony. (Dkt. 24). On October 24, 2024,

Bell filed evidence Exhibits 1-25 (Dkt. 25-39, 42-48, 50-52), an evidence list (Dkt. 40),1 his own witness statement

(“Bell Decl.”) (Dkt. 41), and a party statement (“Bell Party Statement”) (Dkt. 49). On November 21, 2024, ARHA

filed evidence Exhibits 1-12 as one filing (Dkt. 57), an evidence list (Dkt. 56), a party statement (“ARHA Party

Statement”) (Dkt. 55), and the witness statement of Erica Keeney (“Keeney Decl.”) (Dkt. 54). Bell filed a Reply

Party Statement on December 12, 2024. (Dkt. 59). This case is now ready for final determination.
