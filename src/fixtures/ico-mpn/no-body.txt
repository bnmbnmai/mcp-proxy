ICO press teaser
Reddit issued with £14.47m fine for children’s privacy failures
We have fined Reddit £14.47m after finding the company failed to use children’s personal information lawfully.
Failed to apply any robust age assurance mechanism and therefore did not have a lawful basis for processing the personal information of children under the age of 13.
Notes to editors
The ICO issued its provisional findings to Reddit on 8 July 2025.
