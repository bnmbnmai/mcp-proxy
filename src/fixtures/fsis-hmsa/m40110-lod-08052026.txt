LETTER OF DEFERRAL
This letter serves as official notification by the Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) of its
decision to defer, at this time, action to withhold the marks of inspection and suspend inspection
services for the Slaughter Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) process at Collagen
Solutions (US) LLC DBA Evergen (Evergen), M401 10, located at 3413 11" Street East Glencoe,
Minnesota 55336, Consumer Safety Inspector, provided you/Evergen, Jim
Ringelstetter, Plant Manager verbal notification of FSIS’ decision on August 5, 2026.
Previously, July 29, 2026, FSIS issued Evergen a Notice of Intended Enforcement (NOTE), which
informed you of FSIS’ intent to withhold the marks of inspection and suspend inspection services
at Evergen, in accordance with Title 9 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) 500.3(b), for Evergen
Slaughter Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) processes.
Between July 29, 20206 and August 3, 2026, FSIS received your responses to the NOIE, which
identified corrective and preventative actions that Evergen has taken or will take to ensure
compliance with applicable statutory and regulatory requirements. After careful review of this
matter, FSIS is deferring the decision on any enforcement action.
During this deferral, FSIS will conduct inspection and verification activities, as described in the
enclosed Verification Plan (VP). The VP is designed to assist FSIS inspection personnel in
verifying that Evergen has fully implemented the proposed corrective actions and demonstrated
their effectiveness in ensuring continued statutory and regulatory compliance. FSIS will verify
each corrective and preventive action, including whether Evergen meets any deadlines associated
with its proposed actions.
Note: Any changes to your proposed and accepted corrective actions dated July 29, 2026 to
August 3, 2026 must be submitted in writing to the District Manager for prior approval.
