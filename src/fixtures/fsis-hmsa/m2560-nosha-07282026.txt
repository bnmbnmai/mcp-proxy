NOTICE OF SUSPENSION HELD IN ABEYANCE
This letter serves as official notification by the Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) of its
decision to place the July 21, 2026, suspension action in abeyance at Whalen’s Meat Packing LLC
(Whalen’s), M2560, located at 9214 Highway 21, Mott, North Dakota. Aubrey Williams, District
Case Specialist, notified you/Whalen’s/Robin Whalen, Plant manager, of this decision on July 27,
2026, via email.
Previously, on July 21, 2026, FSIS issued Whalen’s a Notice of Suspension (NOS), which
informed you that FSIS was taking action to withhold the marks of inspection and suspend
inspection services, in accordance with Title 9 Code of the Federal Regulations (CFR) Rules of
Practice 500.3(b), based on the determination that Whalen’s handled or slaughtered livestock
inhumanely, in violation of the Federal Meat Inspection Act (FMIA) (21 U.S.C. 601 e7 seq.), the
Humane Methods of Slaughter Act (HMSA) (7 U.S.C. 1901 e¢ seq.), and the relevant regulatory
requirements (9 CFR Part 313).
Between July 23, 2026, and July 27, 2026, FSIS received your responses to the NOS, which
identified corrective and preventative actions Whalen’s has taken or will take to ensure compliance
with applicable statutory and regulatory requirements. After careful review of this matter, FSIS is
placing the suspension action in abeyance.
During this abeyance period, FSIS will conduct inspection and verification activities, as described
in the Verification Plan (VP) that will be sent when completed. The VP is designed to assist FSIS
inspection personnel in verifying that Whalen’s has fully implemented the proposed corrective
actions and demonstrated their effectiveness in ensuring continued statutory and regulatory
compliance. FSIS will verify each corrective and preventive action, including whether Whalen’s
meets any deadlines associated with its proposed actions.
Note: Any changes to your proposed and accepted corrective actions dated July 23, 2026, to
July 27, 2026, must be submitted in writing to the District Manager for prior approval.
