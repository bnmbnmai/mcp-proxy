NOTICE OF SUSPENSION
This letter serves as official notification by the Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) of its
decision to, effective immediately, withhold the marks of inspection and suspend inspection
services for the Slaughter Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) processes at Maple
Brooke Packing, Est. M1745/P-1745, located at 9 Wells Road, New Milford, Connecticut, 06776.
This letter follows verbal notification of the suspension action provided by Mr. Joseph Schein,
Deputy District Manager (DDM), to Arthur Manchester, Plant Owner, at approximately 11:35 a.m.,
on July 7, 2026.
FSIS is taking this action in accordance with Title 9 of the Code Federal Regulations (CFR) Rules
of Practice 500.3(b), based on the determination that Maple Brooke Packing handled or
slaughtered livestock inhumanely, in violation of the Federal Meat Inspection Act (FMIA) (21
U.S.C. 601 ef seq.), the Humane Methods of Slaughter Act (HMSA) (7 U.S.C. 1901 et seq.), and
the relevant regulatory requirements (9 CFR Part 313).
This letter provides you with information on the Agency's authority, the facts supporting FSIS’
action, and information on how you can respond.
Background/Authority

health by ensuring the nation's supply of meat, poultry, and processed egg products for sale and
distribution in commerce is safe, unadulterated, wholesome, and correctly labeled and packaged,
FSIS administers and enforces, among other laws, the FMIA, HMSA, and the statutes’
implementing regulations. FSIS has prescribed rules and regulations required for establishments
producing meat/meat food products, including requirements pertaining to humane handling and
slaughtering of livestock (9 CFR Part 313) and other matters. FSIS has also developed the Rules

Findings/Basis for Action
On Tuesday, July 07, 2026, at approximately 10:00 a.m. during slaughter operations at Maple
Brook Packing (Est. M1745/P1745), the following egregious humane handling noncompliance
was observed. The Consumer Safety Inspector (CSI) observed an establishment employee attempt
to stun a cow using a captive bolt. Following application of the first stun, the bovine remained
standing and sensible. The employee immediately applied a second stun using the same captive
bolt device, and the bovine remained standing and sensible. The establishment employee exited
the slaughter floor to retrieve the backup stunning device (a rifle) from an office. Once retrieved,
the establishment employee used the firearm to deliver a shot that immediately rendered the animal
unconscious. The animal remained unconscious throughout the slaughter process. The CSI
immediately rejected the kill box with U.S. Reject tag #B43-361192 and notified the Plant
Manager and Plant Owner of the establishment’s failure to comply with 9 CFR 313.15(a)(1).
Conclusion
On September 9, 2004, FSIS published "Humane Handling and Slaughter Requirements and the
Merits of a Systematic Approach to Meet Such Requirements" in the Federal Register (69 FR
54625). Subsequently, on October 23, 2013, FSIS issued guidance, titled "/'SZS Compliance Guide
for a Systematic Approach to the Humane Handling of Livestock," to promote a systematic
approach to achieve compliance with the FSIS requirements for the humane handling and slaughter
of livestock.
If an establishment maintains a robust humane handling program, FSIS can exercise regulatory
discretion when an inhumane handling or slaughter incident is observed. Maple Brooke Packing
does not operate under a robust systematic approach to humane handling program.
Based on the findings described above, Maple Brooke Packing handled or slaughtered livestock
inhumanely, in violation of the FMIA 21 U.S.C. 603(b), HMSA 7 U.S.C. 1901 — 1902, and 9 CFR
313.15(a)(1). In accordance with 9 CFR Part 500.3(b), FSIS is exercising its authority to withhold
the marks of inspection and suspend inspection services for the Slaughter HACCP processes at
Maple Brooke Packing, effective immediately.

How to Respond to this Letter
Please be advised that this suspension will remain in effect for the Slaughter HACCP processes at
Maple Brooke Packing until you provide adequate written corrective action and preventative
measures to demonstrate adherence to regulatory requirements. Your written response is expected
to include:
« An evaluation and identification of the nature and cause of the incident.
e A description of the specific actions taken to eliminate the cause of the incident and
prevent future recurrences.
* Any corrective actions, preventative measures, and monitoring activities to ensure
continued compliance.
¢ Copies of supporting documentation and records maintained and/or associated with the
proposed corrective actions and preventative measures.
You may submit your response by fax to (215) 597-4217 or, or by electronic mail
(joseph.schein@usda. gov).
You are reminded that, as an operator of a federally inspected establishment, you are expected to
comply with FSIS regulations and to take appropriate corrective actions to prevent the inhumane
handling and slaughtering of livestock. Please be advised that failure to respond adequately to
these issues may result in additional enforcement action, including initiation of administrative
proceedings to withdraw Federal inspection services from Maple Brooke Packing or other
appropriate action.
