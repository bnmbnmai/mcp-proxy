FDA Briefing Document
Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) Meeting
December 4, 2024

FDA Evaluation of AOD-9604-Related Bulk Drug Substances
(AOD-9604 (free base) and AOD-9604 acetate)

DATE: 11/5/2024
TO: Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee
SUBJECT: Evaluation of AOD-9604-related Bulk Drug Substances for the 503A Bulks List

I. INTRODUCTION

Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act describes the conditions that must
be satisfied for human drug products compounded by a licensed pharmacist.

III. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION

We have balanced the criteria described in section II above to evaluate AOD-9604-related
substances for the 503A Bulks List. After considering the information currently available, a
balancing of the criteria weighs against AOD-9604. We propose not adding AOD-9604 (free
base) or AOD-9604 acetate to the 503A Bulks List.
