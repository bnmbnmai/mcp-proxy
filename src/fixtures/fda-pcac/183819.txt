FDA Briefing Document
Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) Meeting
December 4, 2024

FDA Evaluation of CJC-1295-Related Bulk Drug Substances

DATE: 11/5/2024
TO: Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee
SUBJECT: Evaluation of CJC-1295-related Bulk Drug Substances for the 503A Bulks List

I. INTRODUCTION

Section 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act describes the conditions that must
be satisfied for human drug products compounded by a licensed pharmacist.

III. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION

After considering the information currently available, a balancing of the criteria weighs against
CJC-1295. We propose not adding CJC-1295 (free base) or CJC-1295 acetate to the 503A
Bulks List.
