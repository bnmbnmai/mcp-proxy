   Incident Summary:
   At the time of the investigation, Marine Minerals Administration (MMA) personnel were
   operating under the former Bureau of Safety and Environmental Enforcement (BSEE).
   Therefore, all MMA personnel referenced in this report will be identified as BSEE
   personnel.

   On 1 July 2026, an Enterprise Offshore Drilling (Enterprise) Floor Hand, identified as
   the Injured Party (IP), sustained a facial injury while conducting drilling operations
   on Well B004 on the White Fleet Drilling (WFD)Rig 450 located at Eugene Island Block
   320B. The severity of the injury required the IP to be evacuated from the WFD 450 for
   medical treatment.

   Sequence of Events:
   On 1 July 2026, an Enterprise crew was in the process of running in the hole with 4.5-
   inch drill pipe on Well B004. An Enterprise Floor Hand, the IP, was using a rope to
   assist the top drive in guiding the 4.5-inch drill pipe to the center of the rotary
   table. Enterprise's Drilling Operations Manual is referred to as “Tail-in” when
   tripping pipe into the hole. During this operation, as the top drive lifts one stand
   of drill pipe, the tail-in rope is used to wrap around the drill pipe while the other
   end of the rope is tied off on the choke stand manifold located on the WFD 450 rig
   floor. This technique applies additional friction to the drill pipe to control and
   gradually advance the pipe toward the center of the rotary table as the top drive
   raises the stand, in which the tripping in the hole process can be furthered.
   According to the onsite incident follow-up investigation and Arena documentation,
   while the 4.5-inch drill pipe was being lifted, the tail-in rope slipped from the IP’s
   gloved hands, causing the drill pipe subsequently to swing toward the IP striking him
   in the face, knocking him back approximately five feet onto the rig floor. As a
   result, the IP sustained a 0.75-inch laceration to his forehead, along with minor skin
   abrasions below his left eyebrow and across the bridge of his nose. Minor capillary
   bleeding was documented in the Rig Medic’s statement. Following the incident, the IP
   reported to the Rig Medic for evaluation. During the evaluation, the IP became dizzy
   and vomited. A video conference was conducted with an onshore physician. Based on the
   IP’s symptoms and the medical assessment, the onshore physician and Rig Medic
   determined that the IP required transportation to a trauma center for further
   evaluation. The IP was evacuated by medevac helicopter to Ochsner Lafayette General
   Medical Center in Lafayette, Louisiana. He was diagnosed with a closed fracture of the
   frontal sinus and was released from the hospital on 3 July 2026, with two
   prescriptions. The IP was instructed not to return to work until cleared by a
   neurosurgeon or his primary care provider. On 7 July 2026, the IP received a medical
   clearance to return to work without restrictions or limitations.

   BSEE Investigation:
   On 7 July 2026, the MMA Lafayette District conducted an onsite Incident Follow-up
   (IF)Investigation. MMA investigators met with representatives from Arena and
   Enterprise and collected all available incident-related documentation. During the IF
   Investigation, MMA reviewed Enterprise’s job safety analysis (JSA), Job Description
   and Hazards, and Drilling Operations Manual for Tripping Operations. Based on the
   investigation information reviewed, the MMA Incident Investigation Team determined
   that the incident resulted from the IP’s body positioning and the use of a saturated
   tail rope. The JSA did not address the hazards of using a tail rope for tripping in
   the hole with drill pipe. Additionally, the IP failed to follow the hazards identified
   in the Job Description and Hazards or guidance provided in the Enterprise’s Drilling
   Operations Manual for Tripping Operations.
