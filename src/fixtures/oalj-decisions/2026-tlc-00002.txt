GENETIC REFLECTIONS, LLC, Employer

DECISION AND ORDER AFFIRMING DENIAL OF CERTIFICATION

This is an H-2A temporary labor certification appeal before BALCA. The Certifying Officer denied certification. This Decision and Order affirms that denial on the official ETA file.

The Board of Alien Labor Certification Appeals issues this Decision and Order after review of the request for review. This is a DOL-authored BALCA Decision and Order body, not an OFLC disclosure XLSX.

It is hereby ORDERED that the denial of certification is AFFIRMED.

SO ORDERED.
