PHILIP JOHNSON dba JOHNSON GRASS DAIRY, Employer

DECISION AND ORDER AFFIRMING DENIAL OF CERTIFICATION

This is an H-2A temporary labor certification appeal before BALCA. Employer operates an organic grassfed dairy farm in Wisconsin under the name Philip Johnson dba Johnson Grass Dairy. The Certifying Officer denied certification. This Decision and Order affirms that denial on the official record.

The Board of Alien Labor Certification Appeals issues this Decision and Order after review of the ETA file and the employer's request for review. This is a DOL-authored BALCA Decision and Order body, not an OFLC disclosure spreadsheet and not a Federal Register wrap.

It is hereby ORDERED that the denial of certification is AFFIRMED.

SO ORDERED.
