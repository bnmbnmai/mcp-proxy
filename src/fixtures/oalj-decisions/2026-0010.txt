In the Matter of:
MARCUS SHEFFIELD, Complainant
v.
TYSON FOODS, INC., Respondent

DECISION AND ORDER

This appeal arises under the FDA Food Safety Modernization Act whistleblower provisions. The Administrative Review Board reviews the ALJ dismissal. Marcus Sheffield filed the complaint against Tyson Foods. The Board affirms the ALJ dismissal and notes a kick-out to W.D. Tex.

This Decision and Order is a DOL-authored ARB Decision and Order body extracted from the official dol.gov/sites OALJ/PUBLIC/ARB/DECISIONS PDF. It is not a caselist HTML card and not a Federal Register notice.

It is hereby ORDERED that the ALJ dismissal is AFFIRMED.

SO ORDERED.
