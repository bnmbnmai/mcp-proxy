ORDER ON RESPONDENT'S MOTION TO DISMISS

This proceeding arises under the Seaman's Protection Act and the Federal Water Pollution Control Act whistleblower provisions. Complainant worked as a night watch worker on a pipeline project near Orange, Texas. The record concerns a burn pile was left burning unattended at the worksite.

The undersigned Administrative Law Judge issues this Decision and Order after considering the parties' briefs and the official case file. Respondent's motion is addressed on the papers. This is a DOL-authored OALJ Decision and Order body, not a caselist card and not a Federal Register notice.

It is hereby ORDERED that the motion is resolved on the official record.

SO ORDERED.
