PENALTY NOTICE
REDDIT, INC.
23 February 2026
DATA PROTECTION ACT 2018 (PART 6, SECTION 155)
ENFORCEMENT POWERS OF THE INFORMATION COMMISSIONER
PENALTY NOTICE
Pursuant to section 155(1) Data Protection Act 2018 (“DPA”), the Information Commissioner (“Commissioner”), by this written notice (“Penalty Notice”), requires Reddit, Inc. (“Reddit”) to pay a penalty of £14,472,500 in respect of infringements of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (“UK GDPR”).
Of: 548 Market Street
San Francisco
CA 94104
On 26 September 2025, Reddit made written representations.
The Commissioner indicated that he proposed imposing a penalty of £17,573,750.
