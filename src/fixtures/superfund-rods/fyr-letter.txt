Dear Ms. Butler:

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency has reviewed the Final Sixth Five-Year Review (FYR) Report
for Operable Units (OUs) 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12, at the Former Naval Air Station Cecil Field,
Jacksonville, Florida. According to the EPA’s Comprehensive Five-Year Review Guidance
(OSWER No. 9355,7-03B-P, June 2001), the Agency’s role as the final remedy selection authority at
an NPL site under the jurisdiction of another federal agency or department, requires the EPA retain
final authority to make protectiveness determinations in connection with the site. The EPA concurs
with the protectiveness statements as summarized below. The protectiveness statements will be
reported to Congress.

This review included an evaluation of the remedial actions at nine (9) OUs. OU 1 (Sites 1 and 2), OU 7
(Site 16), OU 8 (Site 3), OU 9 (Sites 36 and 37, 57, 58, and 59), OU 10, and OU 11 are protective in the
short term, but further actions are required related to PFAS in groundwater and potential
dioxins/furans contamination to determine long-term protectiveness at these sites. The remedies at
OU 2, OU 5, and OU 12 are protective. Exposure pathways that could result in unacceptable risks have
been controlled through remedial actions, including through the implementation of Land Use Controls
(LUCs) where appropriate.
Thank you for your efforts in completing this FYR and working with the EPA to include the necessary
recommended actions to ensure that the selected remedies are protective of human health and the
environment. The next due date for the sixth FYR review is September 22, 2031.
