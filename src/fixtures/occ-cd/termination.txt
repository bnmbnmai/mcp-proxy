UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
OFFICE OF THE COMPTROLLER OF THE CURRENCY

In the Matter of:
Bank of America, N.A.
Charlotte, North Carolina

AA-ENF-2026-32

ORDER TERMINATING THE CONSENT ORDER

WHEREAS, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency issued a Consent Order
against the Bank dated July 14, 2022, AA-ENF-2022-21; and
WHEREAS, the OCC believes that the safety and soundness of the Bank does not
require the continued existence of the Order.

NOW, THEREFORE, the OCC directs that the Order be, and it hereby is, TERMINATED.
