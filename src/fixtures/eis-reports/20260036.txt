ABSTRACT
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC or the Commission) prepared this
supplemental environmental impact statement in response to an application submitted by
Tennessee Valley Authority to construct a small modular reactor at the Clinch River Nuclear
Site. This supplemental environmental impact statement evaluates the environmental impacts
of the proposed construction permit. The evaluation considers land use, water resources,
ecological resources, historic and cultural resources, socioeconomics, environmental justice,

Purpose and Need. The purpose of this supplemental environmental impact statement is to
evaluate the environmental effects of issuing a construction permit. The need is to support

consequences, and cumulative impacts follow the NUREG-2226 framework. Alternatives include
the proposed action, no-action, and energy alternatives previously evaluated.
