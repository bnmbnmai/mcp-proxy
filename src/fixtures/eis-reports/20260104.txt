ABSTRACT
This Draft Environmental Impact Statement evaluates the proposed beddown of F-35A aircraft
at Moody Air Force Base, Georgia. The document describes the purpose and need, alternatives
including the no-action alternative, the affected environment, and environmental consequences
for airspace, noise, air quality, safety, land use, socioeconomics, environmental justice, cultural
resources, water resources, biological resources, and hazardous materials. Official EIS

Purpose and Need. The Air Force needs to beddown F-35A aircraft to meet combat-coded
fighter requirements. The proposed action would station aircraft, construct or renovate
facilities, and conduct training in associated airspace. The no-action alternative would not
beddown the aircraft at Moody AFB.
