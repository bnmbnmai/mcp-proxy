                                  INITIAL DECISION

         This dispute by Complainant AMA Real Estate & Financial Services, LLC

 (AMA) centers on whether Respondent NinjaTrader Clearing, LLC (NinjaTrader)

 failed to execute AMA’s trade instructions, allegedly causing it roughly $616 in out-

 of-pocket losses and $2,000 in lost profits. Although this Office has endeavored to

 move this proceeding along efficiently and sensibly, AMA tried to thwart that

 endeavor at several junctions.

         On March 2, 2026, I warned Andy Movsesian, who is AMA’s sole owner and

 acting as its representative, that the litigation tactics he deployed amounted to bad

 faith, and that failure to comply with the directives in that Order would likely

 result in the imposition of sanctions. Notwithstanding this warning, on March 23,

 2026, Movsesian filed a motion for sanctions against NinjaTrader for failure to

 comply with an imaginary 5:00 pm ET deadline by 27 minutes and NinjaTrader’s
production of .pdf files (as required by this Office) instead of native files (as

Movsesian preferred). AMA’s motion for sanctions is DENIED.

        Despite AMA’s attempts to derail these proceedings with filings like its

motion for sanctions, the record is sufficiently robust to render an Initial Decision.

Because AMA has failed to prove by a preponderance of the evidence that

NinjaTrader violated any provision of the Commodity Exchange Act (CEA) or

regulations promulgated pursuant to the CEA, AMA’s complaint is also

DISMISSED on the merits.

   I.        Summary of Proceedings

        1.     Movsesian filed his Complaint against NinjaTrader on June 14, 2025,

alleging that NinjaTrader failed to execute the following trade placed on May 6,

2025: a buy limit order for 2 JUN25 Nasdaq Futures (NQ) using an Advanced

Trade Management (ATM) strategy, with a 15-tick stop loss and a 50-tick take

profit (the Trade). Compl. [Tab 001]. 1

        2.     On September 4, 2025, NinjaTrader filed its Answer, and this case was

forwarded to my docket shortly thereafter. Answer [Tab 012].

        3.     On September 16, 2025, I issued several case opening orders, including

a Discovery Order designed to obtain clarity on whether Movsesian or AMA was the

proper complainant and the damages at issue. Initial Discovery Order [Tab 020].
