                             INITIAL DECISION ON DEFAULT

        Complainant Cristofer Arguedas Asmad alleges in this formal decisional

proceeding that Respondent Interactive Brokers, LLC wrongfully withdrew more

than $93,000 from his account and then closed the account. See Complaint,

Amended Complaint, Second Amended Complaint [Tabs 001, 005, 008.]1

Interactive Brokers acknowledges that Arguedas Asmad had the referenced

Interactive Brokers account but denies that the account ever held that balance and

further contends that key evidence submitted by Arguedas Asmad, including a

purported account statement and account number that Arguedas Asmad has

claimed are authentic, is false.
