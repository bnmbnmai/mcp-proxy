                             ORDER OF SUMMARY AFFIRMANCE

       This matter is before the Commission for consideration of Appellant Himanshu Shah’s

appeal from the Initial Decision and Order Granting Respondent’s Motion to Dismiss and

Dismissing Complaint issued on March 22, 2024.

       The issues raised in the appeal involve already-settled legal or factual questions. Thus,

upon review of the briefs and the record as a whole, the Commission concludes that the result

reached in the initial decision was substantially correct and no important question of law or

policy has been raised on appeal. The Commission therefore summarily affirms. 17 C.F.R. §

12.406(b). Pursuant to Rule 12.406(b), neither the initial decision nor this order of summary

affirmance shall serve as Commission precedent in future proceedings.

       Accordingly, the Commission SUMMARILY AFFIRMS the March 22, 2024 Initial

Decision and Order Granting Respondent’s Motion to Dismiss and Dismissing Complaint.



       IT IS SO ORDERED.
