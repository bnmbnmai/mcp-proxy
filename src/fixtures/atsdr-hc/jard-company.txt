             About ATSDR
             The Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease
             Registry (ATSDR) is a federal public health agency of
             the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
             (HHS). ATSDR works with other agencies and tribal,
             state, and local governments to study possible health
             risks in communities where people could come in
             contact with dangerous chemicals. For more
             information about ATSDR, visit the ATSDR website
             at www.atsdr.cdc.gov/.
