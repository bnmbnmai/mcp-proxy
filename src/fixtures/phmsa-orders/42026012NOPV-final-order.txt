FINAL ORDER

On February 12, 2026, pursuant to 49 CFR § 190.207, the Director, Southwest Region, Office of
Pipeline Safety (OPS), issued a Notice of Probable Violation (Notice) to Navigator Panhandle
Holdco, LLC. The Notice proposed finding that Respondent had violated the pipeline safety
regulations in 49 CFR Part 195 and proposed a civil penalty of $62,900. This fixture is official
PHMSA-authored order text for tests. It is not the 53-column TSV and is not a FERC order.

After considering the evidence, PHMSA finds that Respondent committed the alleged violations
and assesses the civil penalty. The findings of violation, the compliance order terms if any, and
the penalty analysis remain in this official Final Order PDF. The public TSV lists case metadata
only. Operator-response filings are not this document.

This Final Order constitutes the final administrative action of PHMSA in this matter under
49 CFR Part 190. Official PHMSA-authored pipeline enforcement text for the paid door.
