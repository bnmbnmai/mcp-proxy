U.S. Department of Transportation
Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration
Office of Pipeline Safety

CORRECTIVE ACTION ORDER

In the Matter of Amoco Oil Co, Respondent.
CPF No. 3-2026-023-CAO

On August 3, 2026, pursuant to 49 CFR Part 190, the Associate Administrator for Pipeline Safety
issued this Corrective Action Order. PHMSA finds that the pipeline facility requires corrective
action to protect life, property, and the environment. This fixture is official PHMSA-authored
CAO text for tests. Not an operator response. Not the enforcement TSV. Not a FERC order.

PHMSA has determined that the continued operation of the facility without specified corrective
measures would be hazardous to life, property, or the environment. The order requires the
operator to take the listed actions, submit written procedures, and report completion dates to
the Office of Pipeline Safety. Findings of fact and the legal basis stay in this official PDF text
and are not present in the 53-column enforcement metadata TSV.

This Corrective Action Order remains in effect until PHMSA determines that the required
corrective measures have been completed. Failure to comply may result in further enforcement
under 49 CFR Part 190. Official PHMSA-authored pipeline enforcement text for the paid door.
