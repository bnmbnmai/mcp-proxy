                ORDER APPROVING STIPULATION AND CONSENT AGREEMENT

                                          (Issued August 3, 2026)

         1.     The Commission approves the attached Stipulation and Consent Agreement
         (Agreement) between the Office of Enforcement and Regulatory Accounting
         (Enforcement) and Digi Power X Inc. f/k/a Digihost Technology Inc. (Digihost). This
         order is in the public interest because the Agreement resolves on fair and equitable terms
         Enforcement’s investigation (Investigation) under Part 1b of the Commission’s
         regulations, 18 C.F.R. Part 1b (2025), into whether Digihost violated the New York
         Independent System Operator (NYISO) Open Access Transmission Tariff (Tariff)
         Section 3.9.1 from February 17, 2022 to April 30, 2022 (Relevant Period) when Digihost
         interconnected its cryptocurrency load to the Fortistar North Tonawanda LLC (FNT) co-
         generation facility (Facility) without first submitting the required interconnection
         proposal to NYISO.

         2.     Digihost stipulates to the facts in Section II of the Agreement, but neither admits
         nor denies the alleged violation in Section III of the Agreement. Digihost agrees to: (a)
         pay a civil penalty of $45,000 to the United States Treasury; and (b) provide compliance
         monitoring reports to Enforcement as provided in the Agreement.

         I.     Facts

         3.     FNT owned and operated a 55 MW co-generation facility located in North
         Tonawanda, New York that is interconnected to the 115kV transmission system of
         Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid. In March 2021, Digihost
         signed an agreement to purchase FNT from North Tonawanda Holdings, LLC (North
         Tonawanda), a subsidiary of Fortistar LLC. In September 2021, Digihost and North
         Tonawanda executed a Pre-Closing Improvements Work Authorization and Indemnity
         Agreement permitting Digihost to make pre-closing improvements at the Facility,
         including the construction of a datacenter to accommodate cryptocurrency mining. After

         certain regulatory approvals were obtained,1 in February 2023, Digihost consummated
         the acquisition of FNT and thus the Facility.

         4.    On February 17, 2022, prior to its acquisition of FNT, Digihost interconnected a
         cryptocurrency load to the Facility that it constructed pursuant to the Pre-Closing
         Improvements Work Authorization and Indemnity Agreement.

         5.     Though Digihost had initiated discussions with NYISO regarding the power
         source as of January 31, 2022, Digihost did not submit an interconnection proposal to
         NYISO either before or during the Relevant Period in which Digihost interconnected its
         cryptocurrency load to the Facility.

         6.     On April 21, 2022, Digihost President Alec Amar received a letter from National
         Grid stating that the increased demand at the Facility raised concerns with reliability,
         noting that the demand was not preceded by adequate notice and appropriate analysis,
         and advising Digihost to reduce its demand at the Facility or risk National Grid tripping
         Digihost’s load. Digihost received a second warning letter from National Grid on April
         29, 2022.

         7.    Digihost’s on-site load at the Facility fluctuated during the Relevant Period and
         peaked at approximately 30 MW before Digihost dropped its load entirely on April 30,
         2022.

         8.     In September 2022, Digihost submitted an interconnection request to NYISO for a
         System Impact Study, which was necessary for approval of Digihost’s cryptocurrency
         load at the Facility. The Facility has since been accepted as a behind meter resource by
         NYISO. In December 2025, the NYISO Operating Committee approved the System
         Impact Study Report for the cryptocurrency load without system modifications.

         9.   Enforcement opened this investigation following a July 8, 2024 referral from
         Potomac Economics, Ltd., NYISO’s Market Monitoring Unit.

         II.    Violation

         10.   Enforcement determined that Digihost violated NYISO Tariff Section 3.9.1.
         During the Relevant Period, NYISO Tariff Section 3.9.1 stated:

                Any Eligible Customer proposing to interconnect its Load or Large Facility
                with the NYS Power System shall submit its interconnection proposal to

         I.     INTRODUCTION

         1.     The Office of Enforcement and Regulatory Accounting (Enforcement) of the
         Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (Commission) and Digi Power X Inc. f/k/a
         Digihost Technology Inc. (Digihost) enter into this Stipulation and Consent Agreement

         alleged violation in Section III. Digihost agrees to: (a) pay a civil penalty of $45,000 to
         the United States Treasury; and (b) be subject to compliance monitoring as provided
         more fully below.

         IV.    REMEDIES AND SANCTIONS

         12.    For purposes of settling the claims released pursuant to Paragraph 19, Digihost
         agrees with the facts as stipulated in Section II of this Agreement, but it neither admits
         nor denies the violation described in Section III of this Agreement. Digihost further
         agrees to undertake obligations set forth in the following paragraphs.

                A.     Civil Penalty

         13.     Digihost agrees to pay a civil penalty of $45,000 to the United States Treasury, by
         wire transfer, within ten days after the Effective Date of this Agreement, as defined
         herein.

                B.     Compliance

         14.    Digihost shall make annual compliance monitoring reports to Enforcement for two
         years following the Effective Date of this Agreement. The first annual compliance
         monitoring report shall be submitted no later than 30 days after the end of the one-year
         reporting period that follows the Effective Date of this Agreement. The second annual
         compliance monitoring report shall be submitted one year from the date of the first
         report. After receipt of the second annual report, Enforcement may, at its sole discretion,
         require Digihost to submit a report for one additional year.

         15.     Each compliance monitoring report shall: (1) identify any known violations of
         Commission regulations or the Tariff that occurred during the applicable period,
         including a description of the nature of the violation and what steps were taken to rectify
         the situation; (2) describe all compliance measures and procedures Digihost instituted or
         modified during the reporting period related to compliance with Commission regulations
         or the Tariff; and (3) describe all Tariff- or Commission-related compliance training(s)
         that Digihost administered during the reporting period, including the dates such

         V.     TERMS

         18. The “Effective Date” of this Agreement shall be the date on which the

         employees, both past and present, liable for any and all administrative or civil claims
         brought by the Commission arising out of Enforcement’s determination of the specific
         violation addressed in this Agreement that arose during the Relevant Period.

         20.    Failure by Digihost to make the civil penalty payment or to comply with the
         compliance obligations agreed to herein, or any other provision of this Agreement, shall
         be deemed a violation of a final order of the Commission issued pursuant to the Federal
         Power Act (FPA), 16 U.S.C. §792, et seq., and may subject Digihost to additional action
         under the enforcement provisions of the FPA.

         21.    If Digihost does not make the required civil penalty payment described above
