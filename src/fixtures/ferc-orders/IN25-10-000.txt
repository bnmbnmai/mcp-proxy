         (GADS) data1; and (d) oversold capacity and reserves from its Far Rockaway GT1
         peaking unit (GT1).

         I.     FACTS

                 Bayswater owns GT1, which is an approximately 60.5 MW natural gas-fired
         electric generation facility. Bayswater is a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of
         MPH-Rockaway, which is owned by funds managed and controlled by Hull Street
         Energy, LLC. The Commission authorized Bayswater and MPH-Rockaway to sell
         energy, capacity, and ancillary services at market-based rates.3 Bayswater sells all of its
         output to MPH-Rockaway, which in turn resells it into the NYISO markets.
         MPH-Rockaway is the NYISO Market Participant for Bayswater and GT1.

               MPH-Rockaway also indirectly owns Jamaica Bay Peaking Facility, LLC
         (Jamaica Bay), which owns and operates Far Rockaway GT2 (GT2), an approximately
         60.5 MW electric generation facility that is co-located with GT1. MPH-Rockaway
         purchases from Jamaica Bay the output of GT2 and resells it into the NYISO markets.
         MPH-Rockaway is the NYISO Market Participant for Jamaica Bay and GT2.

                GT1 and GT2 share certain equipment and, in July 2021, GT2 was modified to

         on natural gas at the same time, GT2’s gas compressor becomes part of GT1’s auxiliary
         load. As a result, when both units are run simultaneously on gas, GT1’s auxiliary load
         increases by approximately 2.7 MW and its energy output decreases by that amount.

         section 2.4, the DMNC is defined as a measure of “[t]he sustained maximum net output
         of a Generator, or, where appropriate, an Aggregation, as demonstrated by the

                Since mid-July 2021, GT1 and GT2 have frequently been fired together on gas

         that “all generating Resources must test using usual and customary industry
         practices. For example, the operating configuration and fuel mix used to test must be the
