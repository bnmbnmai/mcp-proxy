Report for week ending 09/18/2026
Compared to last report: The fertilizer market was notably uneven this reporting period, with urea being the clear exception as prices
increased consistently across all reporting contacts. Price movement among other fertilizers was mixed with some contacts reporting
increases while others reported declines. Input cost associated with phosphorus fertilizer production have risen sharply in recent weeks. The
substantial increase in production costs is adding upward pressure to the market and creating additional uncertainty surrounding near-term
pricing. Farm diesel prices have surged in the last two weeks, jumping 0.72 per gallon higher than last report. The sharp increase has pushed
the statewide average to its highest level since this report began in early 2022, surpassing the previous record of $4.85 per gallon set in July
2022. Propane was slightly higher.
