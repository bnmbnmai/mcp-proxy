National Dairy Products Sales Report Highlights

Butter prices received for 25-kilogram and 68-pound boxes meeting United States Department of Agriculture (USDA)
Grade AA standards averaged $1.51 per pound for the week ending August 29, 2026. The United States (US) price per
pound increased 6.1 cents from the previous week.

Cheddar Cheese prices received for 40-pound blocks averaged $1.61 per pound for the week ending August 29, 2026.
The US price per pound decreased 1.1 cents from the previous week.

Dry Whey prices received for bag, tote, and tanker sales meeting USDA Extra Grade standards averaged 65.3 cents per
pound for the week ending August 29, 2026. The US price per pound decreased 1.4 cents from the previous week.

Nonfat Dry Milk prices received for bag, tote, and tanker sales meeting USDA Extra Grade or United States Public
Health Service (USPHS) Grade A standards averaged $1.68 per pound for the week ending August 29, 2026. The US
price per pound increased 5.8 cents from the previous week.
