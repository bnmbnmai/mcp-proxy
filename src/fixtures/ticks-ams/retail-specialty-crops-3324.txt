Advertised Prices for Specialty Crops Products at Major Grocery Store Outlets ending during the
period of 9/12/2026 to 9/24/2026
The information contained in this report reflects prices advertised by major grocery retailers to
consumers through circulars and digital promotions and gathered by USDA through a weekly
survey.
New Deals for a New Season

An abundance of fall favorites was featured in flyers this week. Pumpkins, hard squash, apples, pears, and apple cider greeted
shoppers first, along with seasonal potted mums. Cantaloupes, peaches, grapes, and berries were still popular as summer
lingered in warmer parts of the country. Rosh Hashana and National Hispanic Heritage Month provided opportunities for special
promotions, with honey, apples, and beets advertised for the former, and tomatillos, jalapenos, and Roma tomatoes featured for
the latter. Halloween candy, costumes, and décor dominated in seasonal sections.

Total ad numbers this week were 306,807, a 3% decrease from last week's total of 317,902. The total for the same week last
year was 6% lower at 289,953. The total number of ads broken out by commodity groups: fruit 157,419 (51%), onions and
potatoes 25,018 (8%), vegetables 117,216 (38%), herbs 1,150, ornamentals 1,477, hemp 1,913, and honey 3,764. The number
of ads for organic produce was 38,930, 13% of total ads.

The following are the prices of major advertised items (3,000 plus ads) this week, compared to the same week last year.
Significant increases in price for fruit this week included only cantaloupes (each) at 14%. Significant decreases included
avocados at 15% and apple juice (64 oz.) at 13%. The sole significant change for potatoes and onions this week was a 20%
increase for Russet potatoes (5 lb. bag). Significant increases in price for vegetables this week included broccoli crowns at 20%
and cremini mushrooms (8 oz.) at 13%. Significant decreases included red bell peppers (each) at 14% and pumpkins (each) at
12%.
