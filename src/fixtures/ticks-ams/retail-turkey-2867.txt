Advertised Prices for Turkey Products at Major Grocery Store Outlets ending during the period of 9/12/2026 to 9/24/2026

The information contained in this report reflects prices advertised by major grocery retailers to consumers through
circulars and digital promotions and gathered by USDA through a weekly survey.

MARKET HIGHLIGHTS
This week in turkey grocery features, the Feature Rate decreased 2.4% and the Activity Index decreased 36.6%. Parts, ground, prepared, and
variety meat increased in ad space this week, while processed and deli items decreased in ad space. Whole birds were not featured in any ad space
this week. Ground and deli items continue to be the primary turkey items featured.
