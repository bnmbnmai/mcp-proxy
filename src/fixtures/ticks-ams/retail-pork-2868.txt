Advertised Prices for Pork Products at Major Grocery Store Outlets ending during the period of 9/12/2026 to 9/24/2026

The information contained in this report reflects prices advertised by major grocery retailers to consumers through
circulars and digital promotions and gathered by USDA through a weekly survey.

MARKET HIGHLIGHTS
This week in pork grocery features the Feature Rate decreased by 2.2% and the Activity Index decreased by 16.2%. Compared to last week, items
from the belly, shoulder, other/misc., and variety meat sections showed an increase in ad space while items from the ham, loin, rib, ground,
processed, prepared, and deli sections showed a decrease in ad space.
