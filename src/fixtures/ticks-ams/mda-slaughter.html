Missouri Weekly Direct Slaughter Report

MO Dept of Agriculture- Market News    Jefferson City, MO   Oct 30, 2026

Week:  200   Last Week:  160    Last Year: 280

These prices acquired from Missouri producers, Trading as of this Friday morning Oct 30, 2026, trade so far this week and through Friday morning is light as prices and bids are lower with a few Flat dressed sales 3.00 lower at 352.00 with Grid dressed sales not tested with no sales last week at 355.00. So far trade has only taken place in the North with live prices mostly 223.00-226.00 and dressed sales ranging from 348.00-355.00 a wide trading range this week. Last week's slaughter was 546,000, down 7,000 from previous week and 27,000 less than a year ago. Comprehensive carcass weights, the weighted average for both steers and heifers were at 958 lbs, up 5 lbs from previous week and 13 lbs less than a year ago. Choice boxed-beef continues trading in a sideways to slightly lower pattern and seems comfortable trading there as Choice boxed beef is having a hard time holding advances. Choice boxed-beef on Thursday was 1.72 lower at 367.28 and Select down 3.62 at 351.63 with a Choice-Select spread at 15.65 as Choice closed 2.19 lower than last Thursday and Select closing 1.22 higher than last Thursday. Slaughter Cattle under Federal inspection on Thursday was estimated at 434,000, last week 430,000 and last year 447,000. The hide and offal value from a typical slaughter steer on Thursday was 14.69 per cwt down .18 cents when compared to Wednesday 's value.
