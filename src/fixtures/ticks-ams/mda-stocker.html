Missouri Stocker Formula

The Missouri Steer (Heifer) Stocker Formula is a rolling seven day calculated weighted value which combines all Feeder Steers reported by MDA/USDA Market News Service weighing from 400-649 lbs. in the Medium and Large 1 and Medium and Large 1-2 frame and muscle classifications, including all comment cattle. Heifer values are in parentheses after steer values.

Friday, Oct 30, 2026

Today’s Missouri Stocker formula consists of 5061 (4361) head with a weighted average weight of 548.10 (536.35) lbs. and a price value of 419.46 (376.98), 1.10 higher (0.21 lower) than Thursday.
