Compared to last week: Feeder steers over 800 lbs steady to 4.00 higher; under 800 lbs lightly tested and steady to 4.00 lower. Feeder
heifers 1.00-5.00 lower. Demand moderate to good for feeders. Steer calves over 500 lbs steady to 5.00 higher; under 500 lbs lightly tested
and a lower undertone noted. Heifer calves over 500 lbs steady; under 500 lbs 5.00-10.00 lower. Demand moderate for calves with best
demand on longer weaned calves. Several un-weaned calves included. Cattle futures closed sharply higher after a somewhat favorable Cattle
on Feed report last Friday. Quality average to attractive. Weather has finally cooled off some and temps expected to be mostly in the 80's
this week. Rain is still badly needed in most parts of the state. Central Oklahoma has only had 14.5 inches of rain for the year and thru
August the overall average temperature for the state is now the hottest on record. Supply included: 100% Feeder Cattle (56% Steers, 42%
Heifers, 2% Bulls, 0% Beef/Dairy Heifers). Feeder cattle supply over 600 lbs was 65%.
