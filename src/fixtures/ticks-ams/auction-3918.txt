Special Note: Market coverage provided by USDA AMS LPGMN in cooperation with The Ohio State University Dept of Animal Sciences in
Wooster, Ohio; B.J. Campbell, Ph.D.

In comparison to last week, wooled and shorn lambs weighing 40-50 lbs were 40.00 lower; 50-60 lbs were mostly 20.00 higher; 60-80 lbs
were 5.00-10.00 higher; 80-90 lbs were unevenly steady; and over 100 lbs were too few to compare. Hair lambs weighing 40-60 lbs were
mostly 10.00-20.00 higher; 60-80 lbs were 5.00-15.00 lower; 80-100 lbs were unevenly steady. The quality of lambs on offer this week were
of good to very good quality, with local, light weight, bloomy white faced lambs receiving a premium as indicated in the report below using
the terms High Dressing and Fancy. Similar to previous weeks, hair lambs were in heavy supply with good demand for all types of lambs.
Wooled ewes this week were mostly 10.00-15.00 lower and hair ewes were 2.00 higher. Both wooled and hair rams were unevenly steady.
This week's sale was met with good weather, a large crowd in attendance, and moderate to active trade activity. Supply included: 100%
Slaughter Sheep/Lambs (35% Wooled & Shorn, 45% Hair Breeds, 8% Ewes, 6% Hair Ewes, 2% Rams, 3% Hair Rams).
