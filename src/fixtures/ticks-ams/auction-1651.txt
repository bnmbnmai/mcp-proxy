Compared to last week, slaughter cows and bulls were 2.00-4.00 lower. Demand was moderate on a moderate supply. The top slaughter bull
sold for 220.00/cwt. and the top slaughter cow sold for 153.00/cwt. On the replacement side, the top pair sold for 4850.00/pair and the top
bred cow sold for 3800.00/head. The heat dome finally broke this week with temperatures being more fall like with highs in the 70's and lows
in the 50's. Some areas were fortunate to see some rain however most of the streams and ponds are dry or extremely low. Pasture conditions
for most of the area are poor with many reporting they are having to feed hay. Supply included: 36% Slaughter Cattle (82% Cows, 18% Bulls);
64% Replacement Cattle (12% Stock Cows, 67% Bred Cows, 4% Bred Heifers, 13% Open Heifers, 4% Cow-Calf Pairs, 1% Bulls).
