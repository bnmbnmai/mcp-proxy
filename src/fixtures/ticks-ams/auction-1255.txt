Compared to last week, steers under 600lbs sold with a steady undertone, and heavier weights sold mostly 5.00 to 12.00 higher based on
limited yearling comparisons from last week's sale. Heifers sold with a steady undertone also based on limited comparisons. A good run of
yearlings this week with many groups of longer weaned cattle than we have seen in a while, and quite a few nice groups of weaned and
unweaned calves. Demand was good on a moderate to heavy supply. Supply included: 100% Feeder Cattle (48% Steers, 7% Dairy Steers, 37%
