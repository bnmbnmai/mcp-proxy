Advertised Prices for Lamb Products at Major Grocery Store Outlets ending during the period of 9/12/2026 to 9/24/2026

The information contained in this report reflects prices advertised by major grocery retailers to consumers through
circulars and digital promotions and gathered by USDA through a weekly survey.

MARKET HIGHLIGHTS
This week in lamb grocery features, the Feature Rate increased by 7.2 percent and the Activity Index increased sharply. Compared to last week,
items from the loin and other/misc. sections showed an increase in ad space while items from the shoulder and ground sections showed a
decrease in ad space.
