Report for: 09/27/2026 - 10/03/2026

Liquid Eggs:
The caged liquid whole egg weighted average price is higher, caged yolk and cage free whites prices are populated this week, while too few to
report for the balance of liquid egg products. The undertone is steady for whole egg and whites, steady to weak for yolk. Demand is moderate
to good with best interest noted for whole. Offerings are moderate to at times available. Supplies are at least moderate. Market activity is
moderate to active.

Frozen Eggs:
All frozen prices are too few to report this week. The undertone is steady to instances weak with yolk in the softest position. Current spot
market demand is light. Offerings are light to moderate. Supplies are moderate to fully adequate. Market activity is slow to moderate.

Dried Eggs:
All dried egg products prices are too few to report this week. The undertone is steady. Spot market demand is light to very light with
movement centering on previously negotiated contractual commitments. Good inquiry noted for forward 2027 needs. Offerings are
moderate. Supplies are moderate to fully adequate. Market activity is slow to instances moderate.
