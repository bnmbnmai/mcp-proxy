Feeder Cattle:                                           7,474(100.0%)                   4,281(100.0%)                      4,738(100.0%)

Compared to last week: Feeder steers 5.00-10.00 lower. Feeder heifers steady to 5.00 lower. The primary factor influencing the steer market
today was a lack of quality, as the majority of offering consisted of summertime feeders. However, five small drafts of good quality cattle
came through near the end of the sale and sold at steady money. The driving factor on the heifer market today was simply lack of interest.
Several buyers did not participate in the heifer turn, showing interest for a feeder heifer was just not there today. Steer calves 10.00-20.00
higher. Heifers calves 5.00-15.00 higher. Calf quality started out very plain for the first two hours of the sale. As the sale progressed, quality
steadily improved from plain to average, with some attractive drafts offered. Overall calf quality was the best we have seen throughout the
summer. Buyers were very aggressive on average to attractive drafts and were willing to push prices higher to buy calves. Tuesday's supply
consisted of 52% un-weaned calves. Supply included: 100% Feeder Cattle (57% Steers, 39% Heifers, 4% Bulls). Feeder cattle supply over 600
lbs was 78%.
