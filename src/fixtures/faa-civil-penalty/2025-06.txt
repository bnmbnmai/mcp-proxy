                                    ORDER DISMISSING APPEAL

    On December 27, 2025, Respondent John Nuñez voluntarily withdrew his appeal
of Administrative Law Judge (AU) Douglas M. Rawald's initial decision served on
November 7, 2024. Respondent's withdrawal is construed as a motion to dismiss the
appeal. Complainant did not appeal the AU's initial decision.

   The appeal is dismissed with prejudice. The AU's initial decision serves as the
order assessing a civil penalty of $4,500 but does not serve as a final decision and
order of the Administrator for purposes of 49 U.S.C. § 46110(a).'
